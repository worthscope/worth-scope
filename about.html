Our Mission

Turning Readers Into Thinkers

At Playing Books, we believe that reading is just the beginning of a transformative journey. The podcast exists to incentivize people of all ages — especially young people — to not only read but to think and optimise what they read in practical, lasting ways.

In 2013, Ian Crouch wrote about "The Curse of Reading and Forgetting" — the universal frustration of finishing a book only for it to dissolve from memory. We fight that curse every episode. Through audiobooks, active discussion, and deep engagement, we build retention pathways that stick.

The Framework

Four Dimensions of Growth

Every episode is crafted around these foundational pillars — guiding you toward complete intellectual and personal development.

01

Breath

Pause. Reflect. Let insights settle before rushing to the next idea. Depth begins with stillness and intentional space between thoughts.

02

Books

The raw material of every great mind. We curate timeless works and guide you beyond the pages — into the living application of ideas.

03

Thinking

Reading without thinking is accumulation without growth. We train you to question, connect, and apply every concept you encounter.

04

Action

The ultimate measure of wisdom is behaviour change. We bridge the gap between knowing and doing — turning ideas into lived transformation.

Our Vision

We won't stop until audiobooks start having effects on people like music does.

Think about how a song can instantly transport you to a different emotional state, how it can stay with you for years, how certain lyrics become life mantras. Books can — and should — have this same power.

The Curse

Reading Without Retention Is Just Entertainment

Most people read books, take notes, feel inspired — and then forget 90% within a week. This isn't a lack of intelligence. It's a lack of system.

Playing Books exists to build that system for you. Each episode is engineered to give you multiple exposures to an idea — through story, discussion, and actionable framing — creating the repetition that turns information into wisdom.

We don't just summarise books. We play them — unpacking the ideas that matter, challenging you to think, and giving you tools to act.
